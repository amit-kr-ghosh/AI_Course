# Assessments

Keep daily quizzes, milestone examinations, interview practice and research-paper reviews together in this folder. Use descriptive filenames, e.g. `day-03-quiz.md`, `day-07-milestone-exam.md`, `mock-interview-01.md` and `paper-review-attention-is-all-you-need.md`.

## Daily quizzes
Aim for 10–15 questions adjusted to lesson length. Mix conceptual MCQs, multiple-select, true/false, numerical calculations, tensor-shape and code-output reasoning. Do not reveal answers before the learner attempts. Explain every answer and distractor. Record score, denominator, errors, misconceptions and retest needs.

## Milestones
- Day 3: mathematics readiness (linear algebra, calculus, probability/statistics).
- Day 7: cumulative Days 1–7 concepts, calculations and ANN foundations.
- Day 10: CNN, vision and RNN understanding/implementation.
- Day 14: full-course final examination.
- Final mock interview across math, ML, DL, LLMs and AI engineering.

Suggested diagnostic score bands: 85–100% strong working understanding; 70–84% reasonable, revise missed concepts; 50–69% revisit weak modules and retry; below 50% pause and reteach prerequisites. Scores are diagnostic, not proof of professional competence.

## Interview practice
Include beginner/intermediate/advanced, mathematical, coding and scenario questions. Practise concise 30-second and expanded 2-minute explanations, derivations, tensor shapes, tradeoffs and debugging. Record feedback and improvement targets.

## Research-paper reviews
Use the blueprint's eight-part template: problem, prior work, core idea, method, experiments, results, limitations and reproduction requirements. Explain at learner level and distinguish paper claims from independent verification.

## Current AI
For time-sensitive model/release claims, record date checked and primary sources. Distinguish announced, available, independently evaluated and research-only systems. Never invent a release, benchmark or capability.
